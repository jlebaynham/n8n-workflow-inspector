# Privacy policy: Workflow Inspector for n8n

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Workflow Inspector for n8n ("the extension") is made by Jon Baynham, trading as Baynhams (ABN 30 415 213 228), New South Wales, Australia.

## What the extension does with your data

Nothing leaves your computer.

- The workflow files you open or paste are read inside the extension's own page and kept only in that tab's memory. The paste box is cleared once a workflow loads, and nothing is written to disk by the extension.
- The extension makes no network requests. It has no server, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
- It stores nothing: no cookies, no local storage, no sync storage.
- It requests no browser permissions, so it can't read the websites you visit, your tabs, your history or your downloads.
- A clean copy you download is saved by your browser to wherever you choose. A clean copy you copy goes to your clipboard. Neither is sent anywhere.

## Data collected

None. The extension doesn't collect, sell or share personal or sensitive user data.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is published, and the change will be listed in the extension's release notes.
